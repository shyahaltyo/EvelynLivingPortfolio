In April 2026, I created this website portfolio as my final project for Praxix II, one of three engineering design courses in my program, to demonstrate what I've learned for my first year in Engineering Science at the University of Toronto.

But after that was done, I felt a bit somber: I was about to let go of a project that I had spent so much time in...

So I thought, "What if, I could keep this portfolio alive?" As someone who loves sustainability, I'm always looking for ways to reuse previous assets, even if it's digital!

And thus, I repurposed by final project into this living portfolio!
