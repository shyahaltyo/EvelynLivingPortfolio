Why do I do Art?

Although my program is based off of STEM skills, I believe art can still find a place in this field! Just like spacecraft structures are inspired by origami folding patterns, art can find its place in engineering projects as well.

But perhaps a simplier explanation is just that art is fun to do, and it allows me to do something creative! Thus, this part of the portfolio will mainly focus on creative projects I've done with art.

Photography and Graphic Design for Hobby and Marketing

In university clubs, I do a lot of work for marketing, such as Social Media Coordinator for the Engineering Society and Photography Co-Chair for Orientation Week. Throughout these roles, I've learned to be proficient with Adobe software, especially Photoshop since it is what I do both photo editing and graphic design on.

Apart from marketing, I also do photography for fun, where I can practice manual camera settings, picture composition, and editing my own photos! I really enjoy having albums of pretty photos that I can look back and recall memories on.

Finally, I also enjoy digital art! Since each artwork takes a long time from sketch, to lineart, to render, I have to find snippets of extra time and motivation to complete the painting.

Making Props with 3D Prints

A lot of times, I use 3D modelling in an artistic fashion! There are a lot of merch items that I want to have, but oftentimes they're quite pricey, so instead I try to make them myself!

For example, I occassionally do some cosplay, and I like to make the actual weapon props so the entire cosplay feels complete (instead of just putting a costume). I modelled the details on Blender, and the connection parts in Fusion360. Since the printer is not big enough to make the entire prop at once, these connection points are really important to make sure the prop can be put together!.

Other times, I do more practical 3D prints. For example, in my research assistant co-op position with Indus Space, I led a project on giving visually impaired individuals an opportunity to experience constellations in the night sky!

Kniting and Sewing for Sustainability and Gifts

Handmade gifts are often appreciated by many of my friends because they're unique to other crafts. I love to make plushes of their favourite characters as a gift for a special occasion.

Sometimes, I make not only plushes but also clothing! I have donated some of crafts to people in need. It is an act that benefits not just others, but also myself and sustainbility because I have a lot of yarn that is too short for a complete project, so I sew together different patches of yarn to make crafts.

Finally, sewing helps me repair old or unused fabrics. I don't like throwing out clothes that can be fixed, so I always try to find ways to give them a new life.

Future Art Projects

I have a lot of art projects I want to pursue, such as doing another cosplay (March 7th?), making a Flins plushie, and adding engineering patches onto my covie (or leather jacket). But as for now, the main focus in my journey is to complete my engineering degree, so I won't be able to dedicate as much time to art.

However, I believe in the saying that "as long as there's the passion for it, you'll be able to find time for it." Therefore, I'm sure I'll be able to dedicate some of my schedule into these projects - as long as it's not during exam season as a procrasination method!
